Who We Are

Mumbai's trusted
instrument workshop

MK Production House has spent 10 years at Jacob Circle, Mumbai, servicing, repairing and branding instruments for bands and mandals across Maharashtra — from Ganpati dhol-tasha troupes to wedding brass bands.

Every drum that leaves the workshop is tuned, waterproofed and finished by hand, and every mandal gets a custom graphic identity that's unmistakably theirs. From Mumbai to Chiplun and Dapoli, the same workshop, the same standard.
